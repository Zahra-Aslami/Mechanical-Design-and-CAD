## Overview
This repository contains a collection of my academic and practical projects in the field of mechanical design and structural analysis. The work presented here involves a combination of analytical calculations using Python and 3D modeling using SOLIDWORKS. The projects cover fundamental machine design principles, including fatigue analysis, buckling, vibration analysis, and dynamic load calculations.

## Software and Tools
* **Python:** Used for mathematical modeling, numerical iteration, and solving equations related to fatigue, buckling, and natural frequencies.
* **SOLIDWORKS:** Used for 3D part design, creating full assemblies, structural detailing, and generating technical drafts.

## Project Details

### 1. ELELIFT: Forklift Jib Crane
* **Description:** Design of a deployable jib crane that mounts onto forklift tines for lifting operations in confined spaces.
* **Tasks Performed:**
  * Created a full 3D assembly model in SOLIDWORKS, including base legs, main beam, and adjustment profiles.
  * Performed dynamic load calculations based on the adjustable arm length (ranging from 0.1m to 2.5m) to determine the safe working load capacity.
  * Prepared a technical product catalog containing specifications, market analysis, and installation instructions.

### 2. Drill Stand Helical Spring Design
* **Context:** Machine Design Course Project.
* **Description:** Design of a compression spring for a drill stand mechanism based on specific kinematic constraints, stroke length, and required handle force.
* **Tasks Performed:**
  * Conducted force analysis to calculate the pre-load and ultimate force requirements.
  * Performed strength and fatigue analysis to ensure safe operation under cyclic loading.
  * Evaluated buckling stability and calculated the natural frequency of the spring.
  * Developed a Python script for all mathematical modeling and iterative design calculations.
  * Generated the final 2D technical drawing and 3D model using SOLIDWORKS.

### 3. Industrial Shaft and Gearbox
* **Description:** Mechanical design and 3D modeling of an industrial gearbox assembly.
* **Tasks Performed:**
  * Performed dynamic load calculations and gear ratio optimization.
  * Modeled internal components, shafts, and housing assembly in SOLIDWORKS.
  * Applied structural detailing and tolerance considerations for the final assembly.

### 4. Mining Hoist Drum
* **Description:** Structural design and 3D modeling of a hoist drum for heavy material handling.
* **Tasks Performed:**
  * Conducted stress analysis and mechanical design of the drum under lifting loads.
  * Created detailed 3D models of the drum, shaft connection, and winding mechanism.
